01 / How it works

From conversation to follow‑through.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Glasses capture video and audio from conversations.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Argon reads the transcript. The goal is for it to read the video too: whiteboards, slides, notes.

  3. 03

    Act

    Agents draft the email, event, or to‑do. You approve each one.

02 / Under the hood

What runs behind the glasses.

Glasses

Ray‑Ban Meta glasses capture video and audio.

iPhone bridge

A Swift app cuts the stream into 30 second chunks and uploads them. It holds chunks if you go offline.

Backend

Python. Whisper writes the transcript, Gemini 4 Argon reads each chunk, and every chunk is stored as a scene.

App and lens

A SwiftUI phone app, plus a small web view for the glasses display. Ask a question, approve a follow‑up.

03 / The part I built

The Follow‑Through agent.

  1. 01

    Find

    Argon reads the conversation and lists every promise, each with the exact quote.

  2. 02

    Prove

    Code checks each quote against the transcript. No match means no draft, only a flag for review.

  3. 03

    Draft

    It writes the email, reply, calendar event, or to‑do. If a deadline changed, it uses the latest one.

  4. 04

    Approve

    Nothing goes out until you tap approve. Then it opens a ready Gmail draft or Google Calendar event, or saves the to‑do.

04 / Who it's for

Who is it for?

01

Event networkers

It's impossible to remember the dozens of people and slide decks you see in a day. Scene catches everything important about each person, what they do, what you promised, and the slide that made you want to follow up, and remembers it for you.

02

Team leads

Tasks get said out loud in a meeting and forgotten by Friday. Scene hears who promised what and by when, then turns it into a to‑do list and calendar reminders, so nothing depends on someone taking notes.

03 / Later

Service teams

A customer says what went wrong at the counter or on a call, and it never reaches the people who can fix it. Scene catches each complaint, spots the ones that repeat, and turns them into a fix list.

05 / Where it stands

What works, and what doesn't yet.

Working in tests

The Follow‑Through agent on staged conversations. The backend reading a whole session and answering questions about it.

Not proven yet

The glasses streaming into the phone. The bridge code is written, but the hardware test hasn't been run. One real conversation, start to finish.

Not built yet

Video proof for answers, with frames and timestamps. Knowing who is speaking, since labels are set by hand. Sending email or saving events for you.

A second brain that sees what you see, and hears what you hear.
