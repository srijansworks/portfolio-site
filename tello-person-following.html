Person-following on a DJI Tello

Person-following that works, and a delay I haven't explained yet.

Demonstrated live. The delay is still unexplained.

The idea

A Tello finds a person in its camera feed and flies to keep them in frame. Control gets noticeably delayed, and I haven't found the cause yet.

Main build demo (YouTube) ↗

What I built

  • Detection, frame-to-frame tracking, and flight commands from the drone's camera feed.
  • A target-selection step, so the drone follows only the person I pick.
  • A test harness that logs frame rate and repeated frames.

Evidence & demos

The hardest problem

My first delay measurement was invalid

The numbers looked like a stalling stream (26.6 FPS, 964 repeated frames), but the frames were the driver's 400x300 placeholder, not camera data. I threw out the stall diagnosis, and the cause is still open.

What comes next

  • Make the test harness reject placeholder frames.
  • Re-measure frame rate against a trusted clock.
  • Log battery level over time, since several causes look the same without it.
