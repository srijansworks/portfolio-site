Pan/tilt PID tracker simulator

A simulator for learning how a camera should turn to follow a drone.

Simulation only. The motors are modelled, not my real servos.

The idea

A well tuned setting caught a 20 degree jump in 0.61 seconds and overshot by 4.5%. A setting that was too low crawled, and one that was too high shook and never settled.

Open live demo ↗

What this does not prove

  • The motor and delay values are modelled, not measured on my servos. Settings will need re-tuning on real hardware.

    What I built

    • An interactive simulator of a two-axis pan/tilt camera following a drone.
    • Sliders for P, I and D, plus a step test that measures overshoot and settling time.
    • A Node script that runs the same model without a browser.

    Evidence & demos

    The hardest problem

    Integral windup, and a delay I can't tune away

    The I term kept building up during big moves and took seconds to unwind, so I only let it add up when the gap is under 5 degrees. The delay was the bigger problem: higher gains made the shaking start sooner instead of fixing the lag.

    What comes next

    • Measure my real servos: delay, top speed and dead zone.
    • Repeat the step test on the real rig.
