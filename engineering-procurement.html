Engineering Procurement

Turns a parts list into a checked, costed order, and stops before buying.

Built. It fills carts, but a person has to approve the purchase.

The idea

Two Claude Code skills that check each part against its real specs, price the whole order across vendors, and build the carts. Nothing gets bought without an exact human confirmation.

Current limits

  • The worked example uses invented vendor and price data from the bundled demo.

What I built

  • A verifier that checks units and tolerances, and marks anything unstated as UNKNOWN instead of guessing.
  • An optimizer that weighs price, shipping and lead time across the whole order.
  • A purchase gate that only accepts the phrase "CONFIRM PURCHASE" for the exact carts reviewed.

The hardest problem

Deciding what the agent is never allowed to do on its own

It can't swap in a similar part, round an unknown spec up to a pass, or click the order button. Those choices stay with a person, even when the next step looks obvious.

What comes next

  • Try it on a messy real parts list, like a photo of a whiteboard.
