BuildBase

A platform for learning engineering by building projects.

Built and live.

The idea

A learning platform built around projects instead of courses. You pick something to build and work out what you need as you go.

Open BuildBase ↗

What I built

  • A learning platform with two step-by-step pathways: electronics on an Arduino, and CAD in Onshape.

The hardest problem

Making it meaningful and easy to digest

The content has to be real engineering but easy to follow. I tested it with freshman and middle schoolers to see if they could understand it.

What comes next

  • Add more than one entry-level unit for each topic. Some CAD stages have only one or two articles right now.
