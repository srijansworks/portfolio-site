The hardest problem

I expect the mechanics, not the vision, to set the limit

Backlash and servo deadband can make a tracking loop lag or oscillate. I want to measure how much of the error comes from the hardware and how much from the vision.

What comes next

  • Build the mount and get manual control working.
  • Measure step response on each axis.
  • Add the detector once the mechanics are understood.
